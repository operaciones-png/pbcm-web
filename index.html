About us

Closing gaps, sowing the future

In many rural areas of Colombia, deep social gaps persist. PBCM was born to close them through social investment that strengthens communities and cares for their natural environment.

Each project is agreed upon with the community itself, generating local jobs and lasting wellbeing. Because people's development and the planet's health go hand in hand.

Community

We improve the quality of life of populations in the areas of influence, with agreed and transparent projects.

Environment

We protect natural resources —reforestation and conservation of river sources— for sustainable development.

Our focus areas

Six fronts for development

We invest where it is needed most, with projects that leave a mark on communities and the environment.

01

Education

We support access to education and the strengthening of the region's educational institutions.

02

Housing & homes

We improve and maintain families' homes, for more dignified and safe housing.

03

Sports & recreation

We promote sports and recreation as an engine of unity and community wellbeing.

04

Road infrastructure

We support the improvement and maintenance of the tertiary roads that connect the countryside.

05

Water & basic services

We bring water, electricity and sewerage to the most remote communities in the country.

06

Environment

We reforest and conserve river sources to protect water and life.

Environmental commitment

Development that cares for life

Responsible operation and environmental care complement each other. At PBCM, the environmental component is an essential part of every project.

  • Reforestation with native species
  • Conservation of river sources
  • Protection of water and natural resources
